\begin{abstract}
We present \method, a small attention model for translating toy languages.
It reaches 30.8 BLEU on ToyWMT, 1.7 points above the best recurrent model.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Recurrent models translate toy languages well but slowly~\cite{elman1990}.
We ask whether attention alone suffices at small scale, following~\cite{bahdanau2015}.

\section{Method}
\method{} stacks four attention layers of width 256.
Each layer has eight heads.

\section{Experiments}
We train on ToyWMT for 20 epochs, averaging the last five checkpoints.
Table~\ref{tab:main} shows that \method{} reaches 30.8 BLEU.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering
\caption{BLEU on the ToyWMT test set (mean of three runs).}\label{tab:main}
\begin{tabular}{lcc}
\toprule
Model & EN-XX & XX-EN \\
\midrule
GRU & 29.1 & 27.8 \\
LSTM & 28.7 & 28.0 \\
Transformer-base & 30.2 & \textbf{29.6} \\
\method{} & \textbf{30.8} & 29.4 \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}

\section{Limitations}
We only test toy languages; real languages may behave differently.

\section{Conclusion}
Attention alone is enough for toy translation.
